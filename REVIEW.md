# Reviewer guide

The reviewer guide is now part of [`README.md`](README.md): results, architecture, repo map, install, data,
tests, the validation pipeline, the final run, the analysis, hardware and known deviations.

A suggested reading order:

1. [`README.md`](README.md): what the project is, the headline numbers, and how to run everything.
2. [`docs/report/final/report.pdf`](docs/report/final/report.pdf): the final report. Slides: [`docs/presentation/slides.pdf`](docs/presentation/slides.pdf).
3. [`docs/RESULTS.md`](docs/RESULTS.md): every test result with links to the archived tables in
   [`docs/results/test_analysis/`](docs/results/test_analysis/) and the validation results.
4. [`docs/t4_reduced_final.md`](docs/t4_reduced_final.md): what the final run left out and every deviation from the pre-registration.
5. [`prereg/`](prereg/) and [`prereg/FREEZE.json`](prereg/FREEZE.json): the pre-registered hypotheses and the frozen hashes.
6. [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](docs/EXPERIMENTS.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md): every experiment and every note, by topic.
